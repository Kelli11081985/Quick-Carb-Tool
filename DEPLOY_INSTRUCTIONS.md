# Pre-Session Carb Tool — The Consistency Method
## Deploy to Vercel in 5 minutes (no coding needed)

---

### STEP 1 — Create a free GitHub account
Go to https://github.com and sign up (free).

---

### STEP 2 — Create a new repository
1. Click the **+** icon (top right) → **New repository**
2. Name it: `carb-tool`
3. Set it to **Public**
4. Click **Create repository**

---

### STEP 3 — Upload your files
1. On your new repository page, click **uploading an existing file**
2. Drag the entire contents of this folder into the upload area:
   - `package.json`
   - `vite.config.js`
   - `index.html`
   - `src/` folder (contains `main.jsx` and `App.jsx`)
3. Click **Commit changes**

---

### STEP 4 — Deploy on Vercel
1. Go to https://vercel.com and sign up with your GitHub account (free)
2. Click **Add New → Project**
3. Find your `carb-tool` repository and click **Import**
4. Leave all settings as default — Vercel will detect it's a Vite project automatically
5. Click **Deploy**

---

### STEP 5 — Get your link
After ~60 seconds, Vercel gives you a live URL like:
`https://carb-tool.vercel.app`

Share this link with your clients!

---

### Optional: Custom domain
If you want a URL like `carbs.theconsistencymethod.com`:
1. In Vercel → your project → **Settings → Domains**
2. Add your custom domain and follow their instructions

---

### Making updates in future
If you want to change anything, just edit the files and re-upload to GitHub.
Vercel will automatically redeploy within seconds.
